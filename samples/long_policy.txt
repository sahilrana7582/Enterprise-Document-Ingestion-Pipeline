ACME CORP REMOTE WORK AND INFORMATION SECURITY POLICY
Version 4.2 - Effective 1 March 2026 - Owner: Office of the CISO

1. PURPOSE AND SCOPE

This policy explains when and how employees of Acme Corp may work away from a company office, and what they must do to keep company information safe while doing so. It applies to all full-time employees, part-time employees, interns and contractors who use company systems or handle company data from any location other than a company office.

It does not replace the Code of Conduct, the Acceptable Use Policy or the Data Retention Schedule. Where this policy and another policy disagree, the stricter rule applies. Questions about scope should be sent to the Office of the CISO before any work begins from a new location.

2. ELIGIBILITY

Remote work is a privilege tied to the nature of the role, not an entitlement. A role is eligible when the following conditions are all met:
- The work can be done without physical access to company facilities or equipment.
- The employee has completed the mandatory security awareness course within the last twelve months.
- The employee's manager has approved the arrangement in writing in the HR system.
- The employee has been with the company for at least ninety days, unless the hiring manager has recorded an exception.

Eligible full-time employees may work remotely up to three days per week. Employees hired as fully remote may work from any approved country. Approved countries are listed in the Global Mobility Annex, which Legal reviews every quarter. Working from a country that is not on the list requires written approval from both Legal and the CISO, and is normally refused for periods longer than thirty days because of tax and data protection obligations.

3. EQUIPMENT AND ALLOWANCES

The company provides every remote employee with a laptop, a docking station and one external monitor. Equipment remains company property and must be returned within ten working days of the end of employment. Employees may claim a one-time home office allowance and a recurring monthly allowance, as set out below.

Item                      | Amount        | Frequency       | Approval needed
Home office setup         | $500          | Once per year   | Manager
Internet contribution     | $40           | Monthly         | None
Ergonomic chair           | Up to $350    | Once per 3 yrs  | Manager and HR
Second monitor            | Up to $250    | Once per 4 yrs  | Manager
Mobile phone plan         | $30           | Monthly         | None

Receipts must be uploaded to the expense system within thirty days of purchase. Claims submitted later than ninety days after purchase are rejected without exception. Equipment purchased with the allowance belongs to the employee, except for the ergonomic chair, which must be returned or bought out at its depreciated value when the employee leaves the company.

4. INFORMATION SECURITY REQUIREMENTS

Every remote employee is personally responsible for protecting company information in their care. The rules in this section apply at all times and are checked through device management software, periodic audits and, where the law allows, monitoring of network traffic from company devices. The following paragraph summarises the full set of technical and behavioural controls in one place so that new joiners can read them in a single sitting, and each control is explained in more detail in the linked standards that the Office of the CISO maintains and updates at least once a year. Company laptops must use full disk encryption, which is enabled by default and must never be disabled, and they must run the endpoint protection agent that is installed during provisioning, which reports to the security operations centre every few minutes and which cannot be stopped without administrator rights. All company work must be done on company devices, and company data must not be copied to personal devices, personal email accounts, personal cloud storage, messaging applications that are not approved by the company or removable media that has not been issued by the IT department. Employees must connect to the corporate VPN whenever they access internal systems, and they must not use public wireless networks in cafes, airports, hotels or shared workspaces unless the VPN is connected before any company application is opened. Passwords must be unique, at least sixteen characters long and stored only in the company password manager, and every account that supports multi-factor authentication must have it enabled using the company approved authenticator application rather than text messages. Screens must lock automatically after five minutes of inactivity and must be locked manually whenever the employee steps away, including at home, because visitors, family members and delivery staff can all see an unlocked screen. Printed company documents must not be taken home unless a manager has approved it in writing, and any printed material that is no longer needed must be shredded or returned to an office for secure disposal, never placed in household recycling. Video calls that discuss confidential matters must be held in a private room, and employees must use headphones so that other people in the household cannot hear the conversation. Software must only be installed from the company software catalogue, and employees must apply operating system and application updates within seven days of their release, or immediately when the security team marks an update as critical. Any suspected phishing message must be reported using the report button in the email client and must not be forwarded to colleagues, because forwarding spreads the risk to other mailboxes. Lost or stolen devices must be reported within one hour of the loss being noticed, using the emergency number printed on the back of the staff badge, so that the device can be locked and wiped remotely before anyone can access the data it holds.

Breaches of these requirements are handled under the disciplinary procedure. A first accidental breach that is reported promptly is treated as a learning opportunity. Deliberate or repeated breaches, and any breach that is hidden rather than reported, may lead to dismissal.

5. REPORTING A SECURITY INCIDENT

If you believe company information has been lost, exposed or accessed by someone who should not have it, act quickly and follow these steps in order:
1. Stop using the affected device or account, but do not turn the device off.
2. Call the 24-hour security hotline on the number shown in the staff directory.
3. Send a short written summary to the security team by email as soon as you can: what happened, when you noticed it, and what information may be involved.
4. Do not try to investigate, delete evidence or contact the people involved yourself.
5. Cooperate fully with the security team and keep your manager informed.

The security team will classify the incident within four hours and tell you what happens next. Reporting quickly matters far more than being sure: it is always better to report something that turns out to be harmless than to stay silent about something that is not.

6. FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Q: Can I work from a coworking space?
A: Yes, but only with the VPN connected before you open any company application, and only in a private room or booth if you are discussing confidential matters.

Q: Can I use my personal tablet to read company documents?
A: No. Company documents may only be opened on company devices. If you need a larger screen, request a second monitor under the allowance table above.

Q: What happens if my internet connection fails during working hours?
A: Tell your manager straight away. You may use your mobile phone as a hotspot if the VPN is connected first. If the outage lasts more than two hours, your manager may ask you to work from an office.

Q: How long do I have to return equipment when I leave?
A: Ten working days from your last day of employment. A prepaid courier box will be sent to your home address on request.

Q: Who approves a temporary move abroad?
A: Both Legal and the CISO must approve it in writing before you travel. Requests should be submitted at least four weeks in advance through the Global Mobility form: https://intranet.acme.example/hr/global-mobility/requests/new?category=temporary-relocation&source=remote-work-policy&version=4.2

7. REVIEW AND OWNERSHIP

This policy is reviewed every twelve months by the Office of the CISO together with Human Resources and Legal. Material changes are announced by email and published on the intranet at least thirty days before they take effect. Earlier versions are kept in the policy archive for seven years.
